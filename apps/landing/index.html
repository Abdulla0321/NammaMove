Can I use this to travel today?+

This is a design prototype. Journeys, stops and times here are synthetic. Verified Bengaluru transit coverage and production release gates remain open.

Is this live tracking?+

No. This page does not connect to a live feed. Its route choices demonstrate the interface, not current transport services.

Can I download the app?+

NammaMove is in development with local Android testing. There is no public app store release linked from this prototype.

Does this preview collect my location?+

No. This prototype does not request location, sign-in or personal information. Interactions stay in this page and reset on reload.
