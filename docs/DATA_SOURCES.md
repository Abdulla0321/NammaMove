# Transit source rights registry

Audit date: 2026-10-26. This registry records evidence and an engineering eligibility decision, not a legal opinion that all reuse is lawful or unlawful. A public download link is not a grant of every underlying right. A publisher declaration is not proof of its authority over third-party contents. No restricted API, authenticated transport endpoint, CAPTCHA or access-control workaround was used.

## What the ODbL declaration establishes
The [license text](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) distinguishes database rights from individual-content rights (sections 2.2/2.4), offers database copying/adaptation rights (3.1), and sets public notice/share-alike/access duties (4.2-4.7). Internal use does not itself trigger public share-alike under 4.5(c); that exception does **not** clear separate content rights. Applicability to NammaMove's exact contents and output must be assessed, rather than assuming every factual field is copyrighted or that all contents are cleared. This registry intentionally keeps the BMTC eligibility gate unresolved.

## Existing BMRCL evaluation source
The retained [community BMRCL snapshot](https://github.com/Vonter/bmrcl-gtfs/tree/f402c4c69a5d314320024f84620fa83e909c4188) has a [publisher ODbL declaration](https://github.com/Vonter/bmrcl-gtfs/blob/f402c4c69a5d314320024f84620fa83e909c4188/LICENSE), BMRCL timetable inputs and OpenStreetMap spatial inputs. It is an unofficial derived schedule. Publisher documentation says intermediate times are estimated and short-loop coverage is incomplete. The prior import is retained only in the isolated local evaluation database; this audit reads it without new production imports or raw redistribution.

The agency [copyright page](https://english.bmrc.co.in/copyright-policy/) includes prior-permission language and an incomplete template contact. It does not provide NammaMove-specific permission. Consequently the publisher-declared ODbL basis is documented, but full underlying-content clearance for a public BMRCL deployment is **not verified**. Do not equate the earlier `authorization=open` owner manifest assertion with a completed legal clearance. Preserve source/OSM notices, assess rights and ODbL obligations before wider use. Neither this audit nor the previous import establishes an official agency endorsement.

## Evidence and closure procedure
Public evidence: linked primary publisher/agency/license documents, [coverage JSON](evidence/phase02-network-coverage.json), and [acceptance audit](PHASE_02_ACCEPTANCE_AUDIT.md). Private/raw copies of public map images and fetched pages remain ignored, and are not permission to redistribute them. Failed access is recorded as unavailable evidence, never worked around.

To close the BMTC gate, record: provider/rights-holder identity; immutable version/checksum; actual dataset license or signed permission; covered fields and exclusions; copy/import/adapt/refresh/use/redistribution scope; attribution/share-alike terms; expiry/revocation; and service dates/coverage. Store contracts privately, with a non-secret reference in the manifest. Then perform a separately authorized staged import and its data-quality acceptance. No external message or provider request is authorized by this document.
